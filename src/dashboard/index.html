                        Latest from the league

                          How the league works

                          • The bot invents strategies, gives each a name and a personality, and paper-trades them next to your own rules.
                          • Every day it backtests them all on the last two weeks of real prices, then ranks them.
                          • The best get clones: same idea, slightly different numbers, similar personality. A clone of Blaze is Blaze II, then Blaze III.
                          • The worst get a diagnosis (why it didn't work) and retire. Rookies take their place.
                          • Work ethic = how busy it is. Creativity = how different its clones are. Knowledge = how many safety checks it uses.

                          See the last backtest round and the evolution log.
